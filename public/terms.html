Price and payment

Storagebun is pay as you go from a prepaid balance. You add $10 or more, and storage and transfer draw the balance down at the listed rates: storage draws 2 cents per GB a month for the biggest size your drive reached in the last 30 days, and you never pay more than $15 per TB. Downloads are free up to 3 times what you store, then 1 cent per GB. There are no plans, and your balance never expires.

We email you when your balance falls to $2. At $0, uploads pause and nothing is deleted. Our payment provider takes the card payments for us. If the price changes, we email you at least 30 days before the new price applies.

When your balance runs out, or you reach your cap

At a balance of $0, uploads and new writes pause. You can still open, download and restore your files, and nothing is deleted because the balance is empty. Add money and writing starts again.

The spending cap is a second limit that you set. It starts at $20. When the month's bill gets to the cap, the drive goes read-only until you raise the cap.
