Real work on the drive

Three jobs a person already does, each one run through the mounted drive folder and timed. The command beside each figure is the command that produced it.

An agent reads and edits your files

$ claude --print "read notes/brief.md and edit notes/todo.md"
run in the mounted drive folder, one turn

9.01 s whole turn

A 5 GB video opens and scrubs

$ ffmpeg -i Drive/media/cut.mp4 -frames:v 1 first.png
$ ffmpeg -ss 1493 -i Drive/media/cut.mp4 -frames:v 1 scrub.png

0.26 s first frame0.37 s scrub to 24:53

A Blender scene opens and saves back

$ blender --background Drive/models/part.blend --python-expr "bpy.ops.wm.save_mainfile()"

0.48 s open0.51 s save back

Measured 2026-10-02 on the Linux build machine, through the mounted drive over a local S3-compatible storage stand-in: the product's own mount flags, rclone's stock mount. The video was written and read back through the same mount, so its two figures are reads the mount's own cache serves; a cold-read number is an open check. The real iDrive e2 account (issue #173) and the Mac-side halves (a Finder window, Final Cut) are open checks, not numbers here. Reproduce with node --test test/home-demos.test.mjs.

What you get

  • Your files in Finder, on macOS and Linux, streaming on demand
  • Your disk never fills up; the cache is capped at a size you choose
  • A folder your agents can read and write, where a delete can be undone from the storage provider's own versions
  • A safe copy of any folder for an agent to work in, which you approve or throw away
  • A spending cap you set; at the cap the drive goes read-only and nothing is deleted

What it costs

  • 2¢ per GB a month, billed by the minute, pay only for what you store
  • Never more than $10 per TB.
  • No minimum. No plans.
  • Founding member pricing: half price for good, 1 cent per GB and never more than $5 per TB.
  • Downloads free up to 3× what you store, then 1¢ per GB
