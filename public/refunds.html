Refunds and your balance

How your balance is used, and how to get unused money back.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

How the balance works

  • You add money first. A top-up is $10 or more, up to $1000 at a time.
  • Storage draws 2 cents per GB a month from the balance for the biggest size your drive reached in the last 30 days. You never pay more than $15 per TB.
  • Downloads are free up to 3 times what you store, then 1 cent per GB.
  • Your balance never expires.
  • We email you when the balance falls to $2. Auto top-up is off until you turn it on.
  • At $0, uploads and new writes pause. Reading, downloads and restore keep working, and nothing is deleted.
