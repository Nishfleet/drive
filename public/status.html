Status

The current state of the Drive site, read live from its own health check.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Right now

This page reads the health check when the script runs. Open the health check for the machine-readable answer: {"ok":true} when every dependency answers, and the name of the one that did not when one fails.

The health route is /api/health. It answers {"ok":true} when every dependency replies, and {"ok":false,"failing":"<name>"} when one does not, so a monitor learns where to look. A failed dependency is not a lost file: the drive goes read-only before it lets anything be lost.

What stays up

The pricing page, the docs and this page are served from the asset layer, so they stay up when the API is down. If none of them loads, the problem may be your own connection.

What is not here yet

This page reports the health route; it is not a history of past incidents. We do not have a public incident log yet, and we will add one before the drive is generally available.
