Measured 2026-10-02 on the Linux build machine, through the mounted drive over a local S3-compatible storage stand-in: the product's own mount flags, rclone's stock mount. The video was written and read back through the same mount, so its two figures are reads the mount's own cache serves; a cold-read number is an open check. Questions

Short
answers.

How is the bill worked out?

You pay 2 cents per GB a month until the bill reaches $10, at 500 GB. From 500 GB to 1 TB the bill stays $10. Above 1 TB you never pay more than $10 for each TB, counted to the GB. No plans. Your balance never expires.

Can my agents wreck my files?

No. Each tool gets its own folder, mounted on its own key. An agent's delete is undoable for 1 day. On Windows the folder is the one you use, because the agent mount is not proven there yet.

What happens when I hit my spending cap?

The default cap is $20. When the bill would pass it, writes stop and nothing is deleted. You can raise the cap at any time.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. drive runs on macOS and Linux today.
