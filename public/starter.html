How it works

A ready-made notes folder: a vault your notes app can open, a starter inbox and daily note, and one note your agent reads when it works with you.

The starter writes into your own drive, through the same path every other write takes, so it syncs to every machine you sign in on. There is no separate notes product and no second place for the files to live.

Open your drive folder in Obsidian as a vault, or point any notes app at the Notes folder. Point an agent at the same folder and it reads and writes the files you do; an agent cannot permanently delete a file, and one tap puts back anything it removes.
