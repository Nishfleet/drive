Price and payment

Drive is pay as you go from a prepaid balance. You add $10 or more, and storage draws 2 cents per GB a month from your balance, counted by the minute. You never pay more than $10 per TB. Downloads are free up to 3 times what you store, then 1 cent per GB. There are no plans, and your balance never expires.

Dodo Payments takes the card payments for us. If the price changes, we email you before the new price applies.

When your balance runs out, or you reach your cap

At a balance of $0, uploads and new writes pause. You can still open, download and restore your files, and nothing is deleted because the balance is empty. Add money and writing starts again.

The spending cap is a second limit that you set. It starts at $20. When the month's bill gets to the cap, the drive goes read-only until you raise the cap.

Your files

Your files stay yours. We store, copy and send them only to run Drive for you: to keep them, to show them on your devices and agents, and to serve the share links you make.

Drive keeps an old version of a changed or deleted file for one day. It is not a backup service. Keep a second copy of anything you cannot lose.
