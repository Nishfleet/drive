Acceptable use

What Storagebun may not carry or do, and how to report a link or an account.

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Do not use Storagebun to

  • store or share material that sexually exploits children. We report it to the authorities.
  • store or share anything that is illegal where you or the people you share with are.
  • share material that breaks someone else's copyright or other rights.
  • spread malware, or host phishing or scam pages through share links.
  • harass, threaten or expose private information about other people.
  • attack, probe or overload Storagebun, or reach another account's files.
  • open several accounts to get around a limit or a payment.

What we do

When something breaks these rules, we can turn off a share link, pause an account, or close it. We tell the account holder by email first, unless the law or someone's safety stops us.

Report a link or abuse

Email support@storagebun.com with the link. Include:

  1. the share link (it starts with /s/) or the account it came from;
  2. what is wrong with it;
  3. for a copyright notice: the link, your name and contact details, and a good-faith statement that the report is accurate and that you own the rights or act for the owner. We handle these notices at the same address.

We act on valid reports promptly. Report a security problem in Storagebun itself the same way; our security.txt also points at the support page.
