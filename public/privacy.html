Privacy policy

What we keep, why we keep it, where it lives, how long we keep it, and which companies handle it for us.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send account email: receipts, a low-balance notice, cap warnings and close notices.
  • Sign-in sessions, with the IP address and browser name that started each one, to keep you signed in and to let you sign out everywhere.
  • Your files, and for each file its name, folder, size and date, so search and the usage page work without reading the files.
  • Usage and money records: how much you store each hour, your top-ups, what each hour drew from your balance, and refunds.
  • A card fingerprint that the payment provider gives us, so one card cannot open many accounts. We never see or keep the card number.
  • IP addresses, to limit how fast one address can send requests.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. The site does not set tracking cookies. Page counts, when they are on, use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie.

How long we keep it

  • A changed or deleted file's old version is kept for one day, then the storage provider removes it.
  • When you close your account, every key is revoked at once. Thirty days later we delete your files, the file-name list and the version history.
  • After that we keep the closed account's email address and its money records, so the account stays closed and the books add up.

Your choices

  • Copy all your files out at any time: they are plain files in a folder.
  • Delete any file, revoke any key, or close the account from the usage page.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we keep about you, or to correct it, through the support page.
