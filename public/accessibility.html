Accessibility

What we do so the drive works with a keyboard, a screen reader and a zoomed screen, and how to tell us when it does not.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Our target

We work to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. It is a target we keep, not a certification: we have not had an outside audit, and we know of the gaps listed below.

What we do

  • Every page is plain HTML with a heading order, landmark regions and a skip link.
  • Every control works with the keyboard alone, and focus is always visible.
  • Text and controls use the AA contrast ratios from the shared stylesheet.
  • Text zooms to 200% and reflows without sideways scrolling.
  • Animation follows your system's reduced-motion setting.
  • Icons that carry meaning have text, and decorative ones are hidden from a screen reader.

Known gaps

  • We test by hand on macOS (Safari, Chrome, Firefox) and Linux (Firefox). We have not tested every page with a screen reader in every browser.
  • The Files and Usage pages need JavaScript, because they read your own drive. Without it they show their static shell only.
  • We have no published conformance report yet.

Tell us

If a page or a flow does not work for you, write to us through the support page. Say which page, what you used (browser, screen reader, keyboard), and what happened. We treat an accessibility barrier as a bug.
